OUR CORE PACKAGING LINES

Industrial Packaging & Closure Capabilities

Extensive packaging solutions engineered for tamper evidence, gas retention, and global quality standards.

Core Packaging

Tin Cans

High-speed general line tin containers and decorative tin cans engineered for paints, chemicals, processed foods, and edible oils with tamper-evident hermetic seals.

  • Airtight & leak-proof double-seam technology
  • Custom multi-color offset metal printing
  • ISO & FDA compliant food-grade inner coatings
Explore Tin Cans
Core Packaging

ROPP Caps

Roll-On Pilfer-Proof (ROPP Caps) aluminum closures designed for pharmaceuticals, spirits, olive oils, and carbonated beverages offering anti-counterfeiting security.

  • Chamfered & knurled tamper-evident bands
  • Dry-offset side printing & top knurling
  • EPE & PVC-free liner options
Explore ROPP Caps
Core Packaging

Metal Crown Caps

Premium metal crown cork closures engineered for glass bottle carbonated soft drinks, craft breweries, and mineral water filling lines.

  • Standard 21-corrugation precision profile
  • High-pressure gas retention seals
  • Promotional under-crown printing capability
Explore Metal Crown Caps

BKM Industries Limited (formerly Manaksia Industries Limited) is India's premier industrial metal packaging and closure manufacturing company.
