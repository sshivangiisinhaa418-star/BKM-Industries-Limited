THE VISIONARY LEADERSHIP

Basant Kumar Agrawal

Founder & Chairman, BKM Industries Limited

Propelling BKM Industries with perseverance, technical vision, and an unyielding commitment to national self-reliance. From early beginnings in metal manufacturing to building an industrial empire spanning precision packaging, marine logistics, and enterprise software.

TRACING THE ROOTS

Humble Beginnings, Extraordinary Vision

The story of Basant Kumar Agrawal is one of courage, technical ingenuity, and relentless determination.

Early Roots & Determination

Starting with modest resources in eastern India, Basant Kumar Agrawal recognized that precision metal manufacturing would form the backbone of India's rapidly expanding consumer and pharmaceutical sectors.

Packaging Engineering Mastery

Pioneered high-speed automated production for Crown closures, ROPP caps, and printed metal sheets under Manaksia, establishing deep trust with India's largest beverage and pharmaceutical conglomerates.

Heavy Engineering & Marine Expansion

Envisioning maritime supply dominance, the group expanded into cargo vessel construction, pontoon shipyards, and 3D laser scanning services for offshore oil, gas, and transport sectors.

Listing & Modern Conglomerate

Following a strategic demerger, BKM Industries Limited was listed on NSE & BSE, continuing its legacy of high-performance manufacturing, software innovation, and ESG leadership.

MILESTONES & HISTORY

Chronological Roadmap of Excellence

Key milestones defining our evolution into India's premier industrial packaging and engineering conglomerate.

1995

Founding & Initial Operations

Established early manufacturing facilities for metal packaging closures and roll-on pilfer-proof (ROPP) caps, setting industry benchmarks for quality.

2005

Nationwide Facility Expansion

Expanded high-speed production plants across Silvassa, Hyderabad, and West Bengal, increasing annual metal packaging output to billions of units.

2014

Formation of BKM Industries Limited

Formed through a corporate demerger scheme from Manaksia Group to create a specialized, high-growth packaging and heavy engineering powerhouse.

2015

Stock Exchange Listing

Successfully listed on the National Stock Exchange of India (NSE: BKMIND) and the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE: 539097), enhancing capital strength and public trust.

2018

Shipbuilding & Marine Engineering Division

Entered maritime construction, manufacturing heavy-duty steel cargo barges, pontoon ships, and offering 3D laser MEP engineering for offshore platforms.

2021

Digitization & Software Unit

Launched enterprise custom software development, custom web applications, e-commerce solutions, and digital data management services.

2026+

Sustainable Net-Zero ESG Roadmap

Pledging 100% recyclable packaging solutions, green manufacturing energy usage, and expanding social development initiatives for local communities.
