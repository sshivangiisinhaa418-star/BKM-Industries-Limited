ENGINEERING EXCELLENCE

The Mettle of Tin Cans Packaging

Our 25+ years of packaging expertise, automated welding lines, and high-precision double-seaming technology position BKM Industries as a trusted partner for global industrial brands.

We manufacture round, rectangular, and custom-shaped General Line Tin Cans ranging from 250ml to 25-liter capacities. Every tin container is engineered to withstand extreme hydraulic pressures and rough logistics handling while providing complete barrier protection against oxygen, light, and humidity.

Double-Seam Hermetic Integrity

Automated double-seaming prevents leakage and maintains structural rigidity during stacking and long-distance transport.

Direct Metal Offset Decorating

State-of-the-art multi-color metal sheet printing delivers vibrant shelf appeal and corrosion protection.

100% Sustainable & Recyclable

Tinplate metal is infinitely recyclable without loss of quality, contributing to zero-landfill industrial packaging.

APPLICATIONS & INDUSTRIES

Serving Diverse Industrial Sectors

BKM Tin Cans deliver safe, robust packaging across critical global consumer and chemical industries.

Paints & Coatings

Round and lever-lid tin cans engineered for solvent-based paints, decorative varnishes, and industrial coatings with corrosion-resistant interior lacquers.

Chemicals & Solvents

Heavy-duty rectangular tin cans equipped with plastic screw caps or metal spouts for safe containment of volatile thinners and lubricants.

Edible Oils & Foods

US FDA-compliant food-grade tin containers for ghee, edible oils, processed sweets, and dry fruits offering extended shelf life.
