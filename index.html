Precision Metal Packaging Portfolios

BKM Industries Limited operates high-precision packaging manufacturing facilities across India—delivering world-class metal packaging solutions including Tin Cans, ROPP Closures, and Metal Crown Caps.

Metal Packaging

Tin Cans

High-speed general line tin containers and decorative tin cans engineered for paints, chemicals, processed foods, and edible oils with tamper-evident hermetic seals.

Learn More
Specialty Closures

ROPP Closures

Roll-On Pilfer-Proof (ROPP) caps and closures designed for spirits, pharmaceuticals, olive oils, and carbonated beverages offering anti-counterfeiting security.

Learn More
Crown Caps

Metal Crown Caps

Premium metal crown cork closures engineered for glass bottle carbonated soft drinks, craft breweries, and mineral water filling lines.

Learn More
