BKM AT A GLANCE

Powering the Industrial World with Precision & Innovation

BKM Industries Limited is a leading diversified conglomerate with a proud history of operational excellence, engineering mastery, and value creation.

From manufacturing precision-engineered closures and aluminum food packaging to constructing robust cargo barges and providing advanced 3D laser digitization, BKM operates at the intersection of heavy engineering, packaging technology, and global supply chains.

With footprints in high-growth industrial sectors, we align our business practices with rigorous ESG frameworks, ensuring that our operations enhance local economies, empower workforces, and minimize environmental impact.

OUR PORTFOLIOS

Precision Metal Packaging Portfolios

BKM Industries Limited operates high-precision packaging manufacturing facilities across India—delivering world-class metal packaging solutions including Tin Cans, ROPP Closures, and Metal Crown Caps.

Metal Packaging

Tin Cans

High-speed general line tin containers and decorative tin cans engineered for paints, chemicals, processed foods, and edible oils with tamper-evident hermetic seals.

Learn More
Specialty Closures

ROPP Closures

Roll-On Pilfer-Proof (ROPP) aluminum caps and closures designed for spirits, pharmaceuticals, olive oils, and carbonated beverages offering anti-counterfeiting security.

Learn More
Crown Caps

Metal Crown Caps

Premium metal crown cork closures engineered for glass bottle carbonated soft drinks, craft breweries, and mineral water filling lines.
