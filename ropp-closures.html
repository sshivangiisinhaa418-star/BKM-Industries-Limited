CLOSURES DIVISION

Roll-On Pilfer-Proof (ROPP) Aluminum Caps

BKM Industries Limited is a pioneer in manufacturing high-precision ROPP closures—engineered for anti-counterfeiting, tamper evidence, and superior liquid sealing across spirits, pharmaceuticals, and premium oils.

TAMPER-EVIDENT PROTECTION

Engineered for World-Class Bottling Lines

Our aluminum ROPP caps are produced on automated multi-stage deep-drawing presses and decorated using multi-color dry offset printing machines.

Anti-Counterfeiting Security

Slitted and perforated tamper-evident bridges break cleanly upon opening, preventing unauthorized refill.

Custom Liners & Seal Gaskets

Available with EPE liners, Saranex, and PVC-free compound gaskets for volatile liquid preservation.

Versatile Size Specifications

Standard sizes ranging from 25mm to 31.5mm, 30x60mm long closures, and custom deep-draw specifications.

BKM INDUSTRIES

BKM Industries Limited (formerly Manaksia Industries Limited) is India's premier industrial packaging, shipbuilding, and custom software company.
