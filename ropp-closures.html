ADVANCED CLOSURE ENGINEERING

The Mettle of ROPP Caps Technology

Our deep-drawing presses and multi-color dry offset sidewall printing machines process high-purity metal coils into tamper-evident ROPP Caps for automatic high-speed bottling lines.

Designed with micro-perforated retaining rings, ROPP Caps fracture cleanly upon initial twisting, giving instant visual evidence if a bottle has been tampered with or opened. We offer specialized internal liners, top embossing, chamfered edges, and anti-counterfeit security stampings.

Tamper-Evident Security Bridges

Precision slitted retainers guarantee irreversible band separation when unsealed.

Sidewall Dry Offset & Top Knurling

High-resolution brand logo printing, top shaving, side embossing, and tactile grip knurling.

EPE & Saranex Sealing Liners

Custom gasket liners prevent liquid evaporation and maintain gas integrity for sensitive formulations.

APPLICATIONS & SECTORS

Serving World-Class Bottling Lines

BKM ROPP Caps provide trusted seal security across sensitive medical and alcoholic beverage markets.

Pharmaceuticals & Healthcare

Cleanroom-manufactured ROPP Caps for glass syrup bottles, liquid supplements, and pharmaceutical formulations complying with strict GMP standards.

Spirits, Liquors & Wines

Standard and deep-drawn 30x60mm long ROPP Caps for premium whiskies, rums, vodkas, and wines with tamper-proof security features.

Olive Oils & Beverage Syrups

Pourer-fitted ROPP Caps preventing drip spills and extending product freshness for gourmet food and beverage oils.
