CORPORATE OVERVIEW

Building Blocks for a Sustainable Industrial Future

BKM Industries Limited (formerly Manaksia Industries Limited) is a leading precision-engineered packaging and industrial solutions powerhouse—powering essential supply chains of today and incubating sustainable industrial growth for tomorrow.

Precision Manufacturing
OUR POSITIONING & HERITAGE

Focused Industrial Precision & Global Supply Leadership

"Our mission is to deliver precision-engineered packaging and industrial solutions that empower businesses to operate efficiently and sustainably across every industry we serve."

Over the years, BKM Industries Limited has established itself as a premier diversified industrial conglomerate, combining high-speed packaging manufacturing with robust marine engineering, metal processing, and software solutions.

Listed on both the National Stock Exchange (NSE: BKMIND) and the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE: 539097), BKM has facilitated economic growth for decades by contributing to national exchequers and generating employment across its plants and shipyard yards.

FOUNDATIONAL DIRECTION

Our Core Purpose

Our goal is to create long-term value for all our stakeholders through technical innovation, sustainable manufacturing practices, acquisition of strategic assets, and optimal utilization of natural resources. To achieve this, we empower our workforce to drive operational excellence while upholding world-class standards of governance, workplace safety, and social responsibility.

GUIDING PRINCIPLES

Our Core Values

Our values underpin everything we do at BKM Industries. These universal principles guide our decisions, operations, and culture as we expand into new industrial markets.

Trust

We actively foster a culture of transparency in all our interactions with clients, partners, and employees, encouraging open dialogue that guarantees mutual trust and long-term respect.

Entrepreneurship

People are our most valuable asset. We create an enabling environment that supports our talent in taking initiative, innovating, and achieving personal and professional growth.

Innovation

We champion technical innovation aimed at achieving zero harm, zero waste, and optimal resource utilization across our packaging lines, shipyards, and software divisions.

Excellence

We set benchmark operational standards in precision metal printing, ROPP & Crown closure manufacturing, and marine engineering to ensure total client satisfaction.

Sustainability

We integrate strict ESG governance across all plants, utilizing eco-friendly materials, recyclable aluminum, and reduced energy consumption to support a net-zero future.

Integrity

Uncompromising compliance, ethical business practices, and fiscal transparency govern all corporate activities at BKM, building enduring investor and client confidence.
