BEVERAGE SEALING MASTERY

The Mettle of Metal Crown Caps

Our high-speed stamping presses produce standardized 21-flute Metal Crown Caps engineered for high-speed automated bottling filling lines running up to 60,000 bottles per hour.

We manufacture both classic Pry-Off metal crown corks and consumer-friendly Twist-Off crown caps. Every crown cap is lined with food-grade molded liners that guarantee perfect pressure retention without imparting taste or odor to carbonated beverages.

Pry-Off & Twist-Off Formats

Engineered for standard beer bottles and twist-off soft drink glass bottle finishes.

High Carbonation Retention

Resists carbonation loss during transport and high-temperature storage conditions.

Promotional Under-Crown Printing

High-resolution internal laser codes and promotional graphics for consumer reward campaigns.
