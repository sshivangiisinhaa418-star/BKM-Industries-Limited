BEVERAGE CLOSURES

Premium Metal Crown Caps & Bottle Corks

BKM Industries Limited manufactures high-performance crown cork closures for glass bottles—supplying top beverage brands, carbonated soft drink bottlers, and breweries.

HIGH-PRESSURE CARBONATION SEALING

Designed for Fast-Filling Bottling Plants

Our crown corks feature standardized 21-corrugation crimping geometry and molded PVC-free lining compounds for optimal gas retention.

Pry-Off & Twist-Off Options

Manufactured in both traditional Pry-Off format and modern Twist-Off profiles for consumer convenience.

CO2 Pressure Seal Guarantee

Guaranteed leak-free sealing up to 100 PSI internal carbonation pressure.

Promotional Under-Crown Printing

High-resolution under-crown laser coding and promotional graphics for marketing campaigns.
