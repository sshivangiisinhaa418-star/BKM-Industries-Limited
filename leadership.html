STRATEGIC GOVERNANCE

Three-Tier Corporate Governance Framework

At BKM Industries Limited, we operate under a transparent, rigorous three-tier governance architecture:

01

Board of Directors

Sets overall strategic vision, capital allocation, statutory compliance, and ethical standards.

02

Executive Management Committee

Establishes operational benchmarks, evaluates growth targets, and manages business divisions.

03

Operating Business Heads

Executes day-to-day manufacturing, marine engineering, ROPP closure quality, and software delivery.

Commitment to Ethical Compliance

Our governance guidelines ensure full transparency with SEBI, NSE, BSE, shareholders, and regulatory authorities—building enduring investor trust.
